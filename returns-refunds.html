Jonezie Labs policies

Returns & Refunds

We want order problems handled clearly. The process below separates research products from Standards merchandise and explains how to reach us.

Effective October 19, 2026

Start with support

Email customerservice@jonezielabs.com with your order or invoice reference, the affected item, and a description of the issue. Photos of the item and packaging can help us investigate damage or a packing error. Please contact us within 30 days of delivery for fulfillment issues. Do not send an item back until we provide return instructions.

Research products

Research-product sales are final for change of mind, mistaken selection, or an application that does not produce an expected result. If an item arrived damaged, was missing, or differs from the confirmed order, we will review the circumstances and offer an appropriate replacement, correction, or approved refund. We may request reasonable documentation before resolving a claim. Your legal rights remain intact.

A concern about product identity or reported test specifications should include the lot number and relevant documentation so it can be investigated. An opened, altered, improperly stored, or used research product generally cannot be returned to inventory. That does not prevent us from reviewing a documented quality concern or any remedy required by law.

Standards merchandise

For an incorrect or damaged merchandise item, contact us within 30 days of delivery. For an unused item you no longer want, contact us before returning it; eligibility may depend on the item and its condition. Specialized equipment or items shipped by freight may have item-specific return terms confirmed before invoice payment. We will explain any applicable return shipping charge and refund amount before authorizing a return.

Cancellations and refunds

An order request may be canceled before acceptance. Once an invoice is paid, contact us promptly if you need to cancel; cancellation may not be possible after fulfillment begins. If we cannot provide an accepted and paid item, we will arrange a refund for that item. Approved refunds are issued through the original payment channel where available; timing depends on the payment provider. Any nonrefundable carrier charge or return shipping charge will be explained before a voluntary merchandise return is authorized.

Rights under law

Nothing in this policy removes a remedy that applicable law requires. If a shipment or product issue is not resolved, write to customerservice@jonezielabs.com so it can be reviewed.
