Jonezie Labs policies

Privacy Policy

This notice explains what information Jonezie Labs LLC receives through this website and how it is used in our order-request and support process.

Effective October 19, 2026

Information you provide

When you submit an order request, we receive the name, email, phone number, shipping address, products, quantities, selected shipping option, promotional code, and any notes you enter. If you contact support or use live chat, we receive the content of that communication. If you sign up for updates, we receive the email address and any other information you provide in that form.

The website order-request form does not ask for or collect payment-card numbers. Payment is handled through a separate secure invoice service after review.

Information collected by the site

Our site uses browser storage for features such as the cart and age-gate acknowledgement. Google Analytics and our Crisp live-chat widget may receive technical information such as your IP address, device or browser details, pages viewed, referral information, and interaction data. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies according to their settings and policies.

How we use information

  • Review order requests, send invoices, fulfill orders, and provide shipping or customer support.
  • Answer questions, maintain site functions, understand site performance, and protect against misuse.
  • Send news or promotional email to people who signed up for updates, subject to applicable opt-out rights.
  • Meet legal, accounting, recordkeeping, and dispute-resolution obligations.

A phone number supplied for an order is used for order-related contact. Providing a phone number with an email signup does not enroll you in automated marketing texts.

When information is shared

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the site, deliver order requests and email, host live chat, provide analytics, issue invoices, or ship packages. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect the site, customers, or our rights, or as part of a business transfer subject to applicable law. Payment-card information is provided directly to the invoice/payment provider, not to the site order-request form.

Retention and security

We keep information as needed to handle orders, support, business records, legal obligations, and disputes. Retention periods may vary by record type and provider. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your choices and requests

You may unsubscribe from promotional email using the unsubscribe instructions in the message or by contacting us. Browser settings can limit cookies or clear local storage, although doing so may affect cart and site preferences. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, or information about certain uses of your personal data. Send requests to customerservice@jonezielabs.com; we may need to verify your identity and may retain records where law permits or requires.

Age, links, and changes

This site is intended for adults 21 or older. We do not knowingly seek information from children. If we learn that a child provided personal information, contact us so we can review and delete it as appropriate. Third-party sites linked from ours have their own privacy practices. If this notice changes, we will post the new version and date here.

Privacy questions: customerservice@jonezielabs.com.
