A little context goes a long way

Read the result.
Know what it measures.

Match the lot first

A certificate describes a submitted sample and its identified batch. Check your vial’s lot and the report’s sample strength before comparing results.

Purity and content differ

Chromatographic purity is a peak-area percentage. Content reports the measured quantity in the tested vial. A high purity percentage does not mean that every strength was tested separately.

Read the original

Each record links to its source. The original document contains the laboratory’s methods, specifications and scope. These KMD certificates do not report sterility or endotoxin testing.
