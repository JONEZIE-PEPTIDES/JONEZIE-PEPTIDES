Jonezie Labs policies

Shipping Policy

Orders are reviewed first. We confirm the invoice and shipping method before payment, then prepare accepted orders for shipment.

Effective October 19, 2026

Where we ship

The current order flow is intended for addresses in the United States. We may decline a request if a product cannot lawfully or practically be shipped to the supplied address. Some Standards merchandise, particularly large equipment, may require a separate freight quote or special handling.

When fulfillment begins

Submitting an order request does not start shipment. We review the request, email a secure invoice link if accepted, and prepare the order after payment. Backordered items may take longer. If the requested item or shipping method is unavailable, we will contact you before fulfillment.

If an order includes both ready-to-ship and backordered items, we will confirm whether they can ship together or require separate fulfillment before invoicing any additional shipping charge.

Shipping cost and delivery estimates

Available shipping choices and estimated charges appear during checkout or invoice review. The final amount is confirmed on the invoice before payment. The site's general guidance is typical delivery within 3-5 days in the United States once an order has been fulfilled. That is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date. Carrier delays, weather, holidays, and address problems can affect timing.

Tracking and address changes

We send tracking information by email when available. Please check your address before submitting a request and reply promptly if a correction is needed. Once a parcel has been handed to a carrier, address changes may not be possible.

Missing or damaged delivery

If the package is missing, damaged, or contains an incorrect item, contact customerservice@jonezielabs.com with the order or invoice reference. Please report fulfillment issues within 30 days of delivery so we can investigate and offer an appropriate replacement or approved refund path. See Returns & Refunds. This does not limit rights provided by law.

Order help: customerservice@jonezielabs.com.
