HOW IT STARTED

Curiosity started it.
Care keeps it going.

Jonezie began with curiosity about peptides and a growing frustration with the experience of buying them. Prices varied wildly, big promises were everywhere, and finding a supplier worth coming back to took more work than it should.

That curiosity turned into a deeper interest in sourcing, suppliers, and the details behind the products. Along the way, one question kept coming up: could we build a company that cared just as much about the customer as it did about the sale?

That became Jonezie Labs.

Today, we’re building the kind of company we wanted to order from ourselves—with thoughtful sourcing, fair prices, and a personal passion for the research behind the products we sell. We feel lucky to do something we love. Exploring the science, finding new products, and putting each order together is fun for us, and we hope that enthusiasm comes through when you open your package.

We think of Jonezie as a peptide boutique: thoughtfully selected research products, personal service, and a little extra love in every order.
