# Codex Working Instructions

## Mission
Continuously improve Jonezie Labs using the prioritized backlog in `CODEX_TODO.md`. Work on one bounded task at a time and leave the repository in a reviewable state.

## Non-negotiable guardrails
- Do not redesign the site or change branding, pricing, product options, checkout/order-request behavior, COAs, testing data, or RUO positioning unless a backlog item explicitly authorizes it.
- Do not rename, abbreviate, disguise, or recategorize products to evade Google, Merchant Center, advertising, healthcare, pharmaceutical, or other platform policies.
- Do not add dosing, administration, injection, self-use, or human/veterinary-use instructions.
- Do not fabricate GTINs, MPNs, reviews, ratings, inventory, testing claims, regulatory claims, or scientific claims.
- Structured data must match customer-visible facts.
- Preserve useful scientific information, but frame product copy as neutral research context rather than promised human outcomes.
- Never claim that technical SEO changes guarantee Google indexing, rich results, Merchant Center acceptance, or regulatory compliance.

## Workflow
1. Read `CODEX_TODO.md` before starting.
2. Pick the highest-priority unchecked item whose prerequisites are complete.
3. For wording/compliance items marked REVIEW FIRST, do not edit product copy. Create/update the proposed-change report requested by the task and stop that task pending human approval.
4. For technical items, make the smallest reliable change, validate it, and update the task's notes/checklist.
5. Keep unrelated changes out of the same work unit.
6. If a task is ambiguous, policy-sensitive, or would materially change customer-facing behavior, document the question and stop rather than guessing.
7. Prefer reusable generators/validators over hand-editing dozens of duplicate pages when the current architecture supports that safely.
8. Keep Merchant Center eligibility separate from ordinary Google Search indexing.
