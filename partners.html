Partner program terms

Free ground shippingEligible U.S. orders get free USPS Ground Advantage when discounted merchandise reaches $150 before Jonezie Cash is applied.

One offer at a timeThe partner discount does not combine with another percentage promotion.

Jonezie CashJonezie Cash can only be redeemed as store credit on future Jonezie Labs orders. Available credit reflects any redemptions or refunds.

Reward baseEarn 20% on an eligible customer’s first paid order and 7% on eligible repeat paid orders. Credits are based on paid merchandise after discounts and redeemed credit, excluding shipping and tax. Unpaid orders, refunded amounts, and purchases using your own partner code are ineligible.
